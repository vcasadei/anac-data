# Data notice

The MIT license in `LICENSE` covers the code, scripts and documentation in this
repository. It does **not** cover the data files under `data/`.

The data files are copies of public open data published by ANAC (Agência
Nacional de Aviação Civil) at <https://sistemas.anac.gov.br/dadosabertos/> and
catalogued on the Brazilian Open Data Portal (<https://dados.gov.br>). They are
redistributed here unchanged (or split/compressed for size, see `MANIFEST.json`)
for convenience, under the Brazilian federal open data policy (Decreto nº
8.777/2016). ANAC remains the source and owner of the data; consult ANAC for the
terms attached to each dataset and always cite it as the source.
